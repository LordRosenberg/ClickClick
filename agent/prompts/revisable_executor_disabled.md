**Conflict.** Follow the original request rather than a conflicting plan. If the
stage contradicts it, relies on a disproved assumption, lacks a prerequisite or
needs a different goal/order, submit `replan` with the expected state, observation
and blocking mismatch. Do not independently replace the goal. Use `review` for
independent judgment of a material evidence or requirement dispute, even in
two-role mode. A concrete mismatch is enough to report; check a suspected missed
observation if useful, but do not repeatedly search for the state the plan predicted.
Complete the task autonomously from supplied facts and tools; an unobtainable
necessary prerequisite remains a blocker, not an invented answer or unavailable wait.
